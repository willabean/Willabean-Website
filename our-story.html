Our story

A workshop started by a spaniel.

Willabean started with a muddy cocker spaniel and a problem. In 2023, Aimee was training a new cocker called Willow, bright, busy, and allergic to staying clean for more than eleven minutes at a time. Every collar and lead on the market seemed to come with a trade-off: the pretty ones weren't practical, the practical ones weren't pretty, and not a single one held up to the kind of days a working cocker actually has.

So she taught herself to make them. Hours of leatherworking tutorials, a lot of wasted hide, and eventually the honest realisation that leather, beautiful as it is, is not what you want wrapped around the neck of a dog who considers any body of water a personal invitation. That's when she found waterproof PVC-coated webbing. Strong, wipe-clean, kind on a dog's coat, and available in enough colours to actually make something worth looking at. The first collars went out to friends. Then came the steps that would shape the brand: working out how to combine colours into proper named designs, some layered, some halved, some sectional, each one with its own build and its own purpose. In 2024, we opened the doors properly.

What we actually make

Every collar, lead and slip is handmade in the UK, cut and finished by hand in our workshop. Every piece is custom, your dog's neck measurement, the colours that suit them, the hardware that suits you. Nothing sits in a warehouse waiting; every order is built for one specific dog. We make the gear we wanted to find when Willow was a puppy. That's the whole brief.
