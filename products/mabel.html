Two riveted layers of PVC-coated webbing, built to hold its shape

The layered construction isn't just about looks. Two riveted layers of pvc coated webbing make the collar thicker and stronger than a single-strip collar, so it holds its shape through the sort of walks your dog actually has. And because it comes in two widths, the proportions always look right, narrower for fine-necked breeds, wider for bigger dogs who need a stronger band.

What makes the Mabel, the Mabel

  • Bold half-and-half colour design. Two contrasting colours, layered and riveted.
  • Two widths available. Narrower for slim-necked breeds, wider for bigger dogs.
  • Buckle or clasp fastening. Pick your preference.
  • Five hardware finishes. Silver, bronze, gold, gun metal or rose gold.
  • Made to your dog's measurements. Precise fit, not a size band.
  • Matching Mabel Lead available. Plus 30% off your cheapest lead with a collar.
