End-to-end, joined at the centre, balanced by design

It's confident without being shouty. The end-to-end construction means the colour split is clean and proportional rather than busy, one colour for one half, one colour for the other, joined in the middle where the eye naturally rests. You pick the two colours, the metal hardware, and the fastening style. We build it in our UK workshop to your dog's exact neck measurement.

What makes the Mabel, the Mabel

  • Two colours, halved end-to-end. One half is the first colour, the other half is the second. Clean, bold, balanced.
  • A genuinely distinctive design. Not a layered build, not an accent, not a single colour. The Mabel is its own thing.
  • Buckle or clasp fastening. Pick your preference.
  • Five hardware finishes. Silver, bronze, gold, gunmetal or rose gold.
  • Made to your dog's measurements. Precise fit, not a size band.
  • Matching Mabel Lead available. Plus 30% off your cheapest lead with a collar.
