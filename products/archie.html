A combination of colours that genuinely doesn't exist anywhere else

Most collar makers sell you a colour. A few sell you two. The Archie lets you design a collar that genuinely doesn't exist anywhere else, a combination of colours chosen by you, layered in the order you want them, finished with the hardware you pick, and fitted to one specific dog. It's the collar for the customer who knows exactly what they want, or the one who just wants to play with colours until they land on something that looks like their dog.

What you design

  • Your base colour. The foundation of the collar.
  • Up to four accent colours. Layered on top of the base in the combination you choose.
  • Your metal hardware. Silver, bronze, gold, gunmetal or rose gold.
  • Buckle or clasp. Your preference.
  • Your dog's exact measurement. Fitted precisely, not a size range.

What we do

  • Cut each colour layer to your spec
  • Layer and rivet them into a single collar
  • Finish it with your chosen hardware
  • Handmade in our UK workshop, just for your dog
The Archie is our most involved build, and every one takes longer to make than our other designs. Not sure how to combine colours? Drop us a message with your dog's colouring and we'll suggest a few combinations.
