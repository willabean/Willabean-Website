Sectional construction. Every section serves a purpose.

The D-ring and O-ring aren't decorative, they're functional, each one sitting between two sections of webbing to create the grab section, the working slip, and the safety stopper that prevents the collar from tightening past safe. You pick the colours (one, two or three of them), the hardware, and we build it in the UK to your dog's exact neck measurement.

A note on fastening

Unlike our other collars, the Willow doesn't have a buckle or clasp option. The slip mechanism is the closure, the collar slips on over the dog's head and tightens to the precise fit of your measurement.

Slip collars need precise sizing to work safely. Our measurements guide walks you through it, or drop us a note and we'll help.
