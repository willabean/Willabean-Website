Three layers for a reason, not for show

The three-layer construction matters. A slip collar works under load every time it's used, a two-layer collar would do the job, but three layers mean more strength, more longevity, and more confidence when the moment matters. The stopper O-ring prevents the slip from tightening past safe. The grab section gives you instant, one-handed control. You pick the three colours and the hardware; each Willow is commissioned and built by hand in the UK to your dog's exact neck measurement.

Made for

  • Gundog handlers. The grab section is what working dog owners ask us for.
  • Trainers and reactive-dog handlers. Instant, safe handling when the moment needs it.
  • Owners of spirited dogs. For when "just grab her collar" actually has to work.
  • Anyone who wants working kit that looks good. Three-colour layered construction, properly riveted, not an accessory.
