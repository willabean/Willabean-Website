The Walter is the most understated collar in our layered range. Where the Winnie and Mabel are confident two-colour statements, the Walter takes a different approach: a narrow strip of pvc coated webbing riveted along the top of a wider base, so one colour does most of the work and the second sits as a contrast band running the length of the collar. It's the quietly tailored option in our lineup, the collar that looks, on first glance, like one colour, and reveals itself up close.

The construction is the design

A thinner layer riveted onto a thicker base gives the Walter a subtle raised line along its length, a small detail that makes the collar look considered rather than loud. It's the Willabean for the dog whose personality doesn't need to shout. You pick the base colour and the top strip colour, the metal hardware, and the fastening style. Each one is commissioned and made by hand in our UK workshop to your dog's exact neck measurement.

What makes the Walter, the Walter

  • Narrow contrast strip over a wider base. Layered and riveted, not stitched, not printed.
  • Understated two-colour design. The quiet one in the layered range.
  • Buckle or clasp fastening. Your choice.
  • Five hardware finishes. Silver, bronze, gold, gun metal or rose gold.
  • Made to your dog's measurements. Cut to fit, not a size range.
  • Waterproof, wipeable, zero-stretch. Proper Willabean build quality.
  • Pairs with any Willabean lead. The Walter Lead is in development; 30% off your cheapest lead with a collar still applies.

Choose the Walter if

  • You like the craft and strength of a layered collar, but not the bold contrast of a half-and-half.
  • You want a collar that looks tailored, not decorative.
  • Your dog's colouring suits a quieter palette.
  • You like the idea of a design detail that only becomes visible up close.
