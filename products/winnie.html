A solid-colour collar reads as practical. A two-colour collar reads as bold. The Winnie sits in the middle.

Finished, considered, flourished. You pick the main colour and the contrast colour, the hardware, and the fastening. We make it to your dog's exact neck measurement in our UK workshop. It's a small thing that changes the whole feel of the collar, an accent layer of a second colour at the buckle end, sitting against the main colour for the length of the collar. The bit your eye lands on first when you look down at your dog gets a pop of contrast.
