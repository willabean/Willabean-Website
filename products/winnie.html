Why dog owners choose the Winnie

You pick the two colours, the metal hardware, and the fastening style. Each one is commissioned and made by hand in our UK workshop, to your dog's exact neck measurement. The layered construction isn't decorative, it's what makes the Winnie a Winnie. The extra thickness adds strength where it matters, the rivets mean there's nothing to unravel or wear through, and the two colours mean no other dog has the same collar as yours. It rinses clean under a tap, shrugs off the mud, and lasts through the kind of walks your dog deserves.
