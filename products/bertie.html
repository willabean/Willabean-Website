The quietest collar we make

Because the Bertie opens and closes through the O-ring rather than a buckle or clasp, it's quick to slip on in the morning and off at night. There's no hardware to scratch the sofa or clink at two in the morning. It's the quietest collar we make, for the quietest part of your dog's day.

Please read before ordering, the Bertie is not a walking collar

  • No lead attachment. The O-ring design isn't built to hold a lead or absorb a pulling load.
  • Designed for indoor wear. Not for outdoor walks, recall situations, or any moment your dog might pull.
  • Pairs with our walking collars. Most Bertie customers also have a Hugo, Winnie or other Willabean walking collar for going out, and use the Bertie at home.

Why a house collar at all?

  • Comfort first. Lighter and softer than a walking collar, perfect for long indoor hours.
  • No hardware against fur. Nothing to catch on blankets, crates or furniture.
  • ID-tag friendly. A place for their tag at home, when they're not on a lead.
  • Still made for them. Cut to their neck measurement, in the colour you pick.
