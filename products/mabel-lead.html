Properly built rather than simply cut

Like the collar, the Mabel Lead is properly built rather than simply cut. The two layers add thickness where your hand wants it, a grip that sits comfortably on a long walk, not a thin strip that digs in after ten minutes. The rivets hold the construction together through years of walking, and the pvc coated webbing shrugs off mud, rain and the occasional drop in a puddle.
