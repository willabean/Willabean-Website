One colour for the half closest to your dog. The other for the half in your hand.

Like the collar, the Mabel Lead is properly built rather than simply cut. The clean centre join is what makes it look intentional rather than busy, one colour for the half closest to your dog, the other for the half in your hand, with a clear line between them. The PVC-coated webbing shrugs off mud, rain and the occasional drop in a puddle.
