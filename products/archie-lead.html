Match your Archie collar, or don't

You can match your Archie collar exactly, same base, same layered colours, same order, for a set that's genuinely unique as a pair. Or you can use the lead as a chance to play with a slightly different combination, so the collar and lead speak to each other without being identical twins. Either way, it's a lead that doesn't exist anywhere else on any other dog.

What you design

  • Your base colour. The foundation of the lead.
  • Up to four accent colours. Layered on top in the order you choose.
  • Your length and hardware. 1.2m, 1.5m or 1.8m in silver, bronze, gold, gunmetal or rose gold.
  • Buckle- or clasp-compatible. Works with both Archie collar fastenings.

What we do

  • Cut each colour layer to your spec
  • Layer and rivet the lead to match your design
  • Finish it with your chosen hardware
  • Handmade in our UK workshop, to your length
