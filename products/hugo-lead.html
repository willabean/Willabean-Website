Feels right after an hour in your hand

Because it's PVC-coated webbing, the Hugo Lead doesn't soak up rain like a fabric lead or crack like a cheap leather one. It wipes clean under a tap, doesn't hold smells, and stays flexible in the cold. The metal hardware holds, the rivets are set properly, and the handle sits comfortably in a hand that's been wrapped around it for an hour. These sound like small things until you've walked a dog in the rain with a lead that wasn't built for it.
