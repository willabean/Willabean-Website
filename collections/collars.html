Dog collars · Handmade in the UK

Collars designed by you. Made by us.

Every collar in our range is handmade in our UK workshop, made to your dog's exact neck measurement, and finished in the colours and hardware you choose. What makes each design different is how it's built. Our single-layer collars are clean and classic. Our layered collars take two, three or four colours of pvc coated webbing and rivet them together into something thicker, stronger, and genuinely one-of-one. Here's the full range.
