What is PVC-coated webbing, and why your next dog collar should be made of it

If you've ever walked a wet dog home and caught the smell of last week's pond off their collar, this post is for you.

The everyday problem

Most dog owners learn this the hard way. You buy a nice leather collar. Your dog swims in a river. The collar comes out wet, heavy, dark around the stitching, and three weeks later it smells like something died in it. Or you try a nylon one, better with water, but it frays at the buckle, soaks up mud, and fades in the sun. Neither material is actually designed for the life a real walking dog leads.

PVC coated webbing is. It's the reason every collar we make is built from it.

What PVC-coated webbing actually is

PVC coated webbing is an engineered webbing. a woven polyester core (strong, low-stretch, load-bearing) bonded with a thin coating of TPU or PVC (waterproof, wipeable, flexible). The polyester core gives it strength comparable to leather; the coating gives it the feel and behaviour of a modern technical fabric. You get the best of both: soft enough to wrap cleanly around a dog's neck, tough enough to survive being bitten by a beagle, and completely indifferent to water.

Its pedigree

PVC coated webbing isn't new, and it wasn't invented for dogs. It was originally developed for industrial and equestrian use, horse tack, military gear, marine rope. Anywhere you need a strap that can get soaked, muddy, salted, and still hold load for years. Dog collar makers adopted it because the properties that make it right for a working horse also make it right for a working spaniel.

Five reasons it beats leather for active dogs

  1. Waterproof. PVC coated webbing doesn't absorb water. A soaked leather collar is heavy, dark, and takes a day to dry; a soaked pvc coated webbing collar wipes clean and is ready for the next walk.
  2. Doesn't absorb smell. Leather smells are a one-way door. PVC coated webbing doesn't hold odour, because there's no porous surface for bacteria to live in.
  3. Doesn't stretch when wet. Leather stretches as it soaks, then shrinks as it dries. Repeatedly. A collar that fit perfectly in month one doesn't in month six. PVC coated webbing holds its dimension.
  4. Wipes clean. A damp cloth is all you need. No waxing, no oiling, no leather conditioner.
  5. Bright colours stay bright. UV and water don't fade pvc coated webbing the way they do dyed leather. Three years in, the colour is still the colour.

The fair comparison

PVC coated webbing doesn't win on everything. Here's the honest version:

vs leather

Leather ages with character, a well-oiled leather collar develops a patina pvc coated webbing never will. If your dog walks dry and you like that lived-in look, leather is lovely. But most active UK dogs don't walk dry for months at a time, and a leather collar ages badly in rain. PVC coated webbing wins for function; leather wins for aesthetics if you value patina.

vs nylon webbing

Nylon is cheap and light but frays, holds water, stretches, and fades fast. It's fine for a budget collar for a short-term use. For a collar you want to still love after three winters, pvc coated webbing outlasts it comfortably.

vs rope / paracord

Rope collars are popular with gundog handlers for good reason, excellent grip, no hardware. But they're hard to clean once dirty, and they can tangle on feathered coats. PVC coated webbing slips are cleaner-looking and wipe down in seconds, which is why our Willow uses pvc coated webbing even though most slip collars are rope.

Is it safe? Kind on fur?

Yes and yes. PVC coated webbing is FDA-approved for food contact, hypoallergenic, and has a smooth surface that doesn't catch or pull on fur. Many of the owners who switch to pvc coated webbing do it because their dog was reacting to something, nylon, leather oils, metal hardware sitting against damp fabric, and the problem goes away once the collar changes.

Care

Basically nothing. Wipe with a damp cloth after muddy walks. Rinse under the tap if it's particularly bad. That's the whole maintenance routine. No waxing, no conditioning, no leaving it on the radiator overnight to dry. This is why pvc coated webbing is the material of choice for owners who walk their dogs in the British weather, which is to say, in wet weather, which is to say, most of us.

The honest part about longevity and chewing

PVC coated webbing is tough, but it's not indestructible. A determined chewer will get through a pvc coated webbing collar the same way they'd get through a leather one, just slower. If your dog habitually chews their own collar, no collar material solves that problem; training does. That said, rivets hold better than stitching against a chewing mouth, which is why every layered Willabean is riveted rather than sewn.

Why we layer and rivet it

A single layer of pvc coated webbing is plenty strong for a walking collar. But two or three layers, riveted together, make something meaningfully different, thicker, with more structural integrity, and with a visual depth that a single strip doesn't have. It's what turns a functional walking collar into a considered design object. Every layered Willabean, the Walter, Winnie, Mabel, Willow and Archie, is built this way.

Frequently asked questions

Is PVC-coated webbing plastic?

Not quite. PVC coated webbing is a polyester webbing core (the part that takes the load) bonded with a thin TPU or PVC coating. The coating is plastic-like, that's what makes it waterproof and wipeable, but the structural core is woven polyester, same as a seatbelt.

Is PVC-coated webbing safe for dogs?

Yes. PVC coated webbing is FDA-approved for direct food contact, hypoallergenic, and has a smooth surface that's kind on fur. Many owners of dogs with skin issues or fabric allergies switch to pvc coated webbing for exactly this reason.

How long does a PVC-coated webbing collar last?

Properly built pvc coated webbing collars last years, often longer than leather, because they don't dry out, crack, or absorb moisture. The hardware usually fails before the pvc coated webbing does.

Can PVC-coated webbing collars be personalised?

PVC coated webbing doesn't take embossing the way leather does. For personalisation, attach an engraved ID tag to the D-ring, that's the convention and what most of our customers do.

Why is PVC-coated webbing more expensive than nylon?

Nylon webbing is mass-produced cheaply and stitched with a sewing machine. PVC coated webbing, especially layered, riveted pvc coated webbing, requires thicker material, proper rivet work, and more cutting and finishing time. You're paying for build quality and material, not branding.

Does PVC-coated webbing smell?

No. One of pvc coated webbing's main advantages over leather, nylon and rope is that it doesn't absorb smells. Wipe it clean after a muddy walk and it smells like it did when you bought it.
