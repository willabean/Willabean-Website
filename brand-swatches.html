Quick reference

For print and embroidery — Pantone-equivalent matches are a printer's job, but the closest standards are:
· Moss #3E5236 — close to Pantone 5747 C
· Bronze #A67B40 — close to Pantone 4505 C or a real bronze foil for hot-stamping
· Cream #F5EDE0 — close to Pantone 9183 C (or uncoated card)
· Terracotta #C2592E — close to Pantone 173 C

For social / digital — use the hex values above directly. Backgrounds: cream. Headlines: Moss Deep. Body: Ink. Accents: Bronze.

Typefaces — both free on Google Fonts. Headlines/logo: Fraunces (medium 500, occasional italic for the brand mark). Body: Inter (400 regular, 600 for emphasis).
