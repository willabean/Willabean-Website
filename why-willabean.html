Why Willabean

The same materials. A different way of making them.

A lot of UK brands sell PVC-coated webbing dog collars. What you actually get for your money differs more than you'd think. Here's the honest version.

Five things you only get with us

01

Layered and riveted, not stitched or printed.

Most two-tone collars are either a single strip with a contrast stripe printed on, or two strips stitched together. Ours are layered: two, three, even seven separate strips of webbing, riveted at intervals along the length. The result is thicker, stronger, won't unravel and genuinely one-of-a-kind. It's a slower way to build a collar, and you can see the difference in your hand.

02

Made to your dog's neck, not a size band.

You give us a measurement in centimetres, we cut to that figure. Not "small/medium/large", not "fits 32-40cm". The actual number. It's why every Willabean collar is made to order rather than picked off a shelf, and why the lead time is measured in days rather than minutes. How to measure →

03

19 webbing colours, 5 hardware finishes, your call.

Not three "popular" colours. Not "we'll let you know what's in stock". The full range, every time, on every layered design. Silver, bronze, gold, gunmetal or rose gold metalwork to match.

04

Designed by us. Made by us. Sent by us.

Every collar passes through one of our hands before it ships. We're not a marketplace, not a print-on-demand operation, and we don't outsource the build. When you email us, you get one of us back, usually the same day, usually with Willow asleep under the desk.

05

30% off the lead with every collar.

Buy a collar and a lead in the same order, your cheapest lead drops by 30% automatically at checkout. One discount per collar, no code, no offer window. It's just the price for buying a set. We'd rather customers get the whole kit than nickel-and-dime the discount.
