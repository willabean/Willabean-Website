Returns & Refunds

Last updated: 25 April 2026

The short version. Bespoke handmade collars and leads are non-returnable once we've started making them, because they're cut to your dog. Stocked branded products (Mud Daddy, Sporting Saint dummies) can be returned within 14 days for a refund, provided they're unused and in their original packaging. Anything faulty: email us, we'll sort it.

1. Bespoke / handmade products

Every Willabean collar, lead and slip is handmade to order, cut to your dog's exact neck measurement, and built in the colour combination you've chosen. Because they are personalised to you, the standard 14-day cancellation right under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 does not apply once we have started making the item (Regulation 28(1)(b)).

You may cancel a bespoke order up until the point we begin making it, by emailing team@willabean.co.uk. We aim to begin builds within a few working days, so cancel quickly if you change your mind.

If a bespoke item is faulty, mis-described, or not of satisfactory quality, your statutory rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 still apply. See "Faulty items" below.

2. Stocked branded products

Branded items we stock but do not make ourselves (Mud Daddy, Sporting Saint training equipment, etc.) are not personalised. Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, you have the right to cancel within 14 days of receiving the goods, for any reason.

To exercise this right:

  1. Email team@willabean.co.uk within 14 days of delivery, telling us you want to cancel.
  2. Return the item to us within a further 14 days, unused and in its original packaging.
  3. We refund the price of the goods within 14 days of receiving them back, or proof of return.

The cost of return postage is yours unless the item is faulty. We recommend a tracked service, as we can only refund items we've received back.

3. Faulty items

If anything you've ordered from us turns out to be faulty, mis-described, or not of satisfactory quality, your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 apply. In short:

  • Within 30 days, you have the right to a full refund.
  • Within six months, you have the right to a repair, replacement or refund. After one failed attempt at repair or replacement, you can ask for a refund.
  • After six months, you may still have rights to a partial refund or repair, depending on the nature of the fault.

To report a fault, email team@willabean.co.uk with your order reference and a couple of photos showing the issue. We'll come back to you within a few working days. If the item is genuinely faulty, we cover the cost of return postage.

4. Damage in transit

If your order arrives visibly damaged, please take a photo of the packaging and the item before unpacking further if possible, and email us within 48 hours. We'll arrange a replacement or refund.

5. How refunds are paid

Refunds are paid back to the original payment method used at checkout. They typically appear within 3 to 5 working days of us issuing the refund, though this depends on your bank or card provider.

6. Items not eligible for return

For hygiene reasons, we cannot accept returns of items that have been worn by a dog or used in training, except where the item is faulty.

7. Your statutory rights

None of the above limits your statutory rights as a consumer. If you have any doubts, you can find independent guidance at Citizens Advice.

Need help? Email team@willabean.co.uk with your order number and we'll come back to you, usually the same day.
