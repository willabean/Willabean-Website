Who we are

Willabean is a trading name of Willabean Ltd, a company registered in England, company number 15194903. Our registered office is 50 Princes Street, Ipswich, Suffolk, IP1 1RJ.

In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" refer to Willabean Ltd, the data controller of the personal information you share with us. You can reach us by email at team@willabean.co.uk, or by post at the address above.

This policy explains, in plain English, what personal data we collect, why, how we use and store it, and the rights you have over it. It is written in line with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What information we collect

When you place an order or contact us, we collect:

  • Contact details, name, email address, phone number (if provided), billing address and shipping address.
  • Order information, the products you ordered, including any custom details such as your dog's neck measurement and chosen colour combination.
  • Payment information, we do not store full card numbers. Payment is handled by our payment processor (Shopify Payments and / or other supported gateways), who collect card and billing data directly. We receive only the confirmation of payment and a reference.
  • Communications, the content of any emails or messages you send us, so we can respond and keep a record.

When you visit the website, our hosting platform (Shopify) and any analytics tools we use also collect:

  • Device and browsing data, IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring page, session timing.
  • Cookies, small text files used for the basket, login state and (where you have consented) analytics. See "Cookies" below.

Why we collect it (lawful basis)

We process your personal data for these reasons, on the lawful bases shown:

  • To fulfil your order, processing payment, building your collar, dispatching it, providing tracking and support. Lawful basis: performance of a contract.
  • To respond to your enquiries, replying to emails or messages. Lawful basis: legitimate interests (running the business and helping customers).
  • To meet legal obligations, tax and accounting records, statutory complaint handling. Lawful basis: legal obligation.
  • To send marketing, only if you have asked us to (for example by signing up to our newsletter). Lawful basis: consent. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • To run and improve the website, including analytics, fraud prevention and security. Lawful basis: legitimate interests or consent, depending on the cookie or tool involved.

Who we share it with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only with the providers we need to run the business, and only as much as they need to do their job:

  • Shopify, our e-commerce platform and payment processor. Hosts the shop, takes payment and stores order records.
  • Royal Mail and other couriers, to deliver your order. They receive your name and shipping address.
  • Email service provider, to send order confirmations, shipping updates and (with your consent) marketing emails.
  • Accountancy and tax services, where we need to keep records for HMRC compliance.
  • Law enforcement and regulators, where required by law (for example, in response to a court order).

We choose providers based outside the UK only where they offer adequate data-protection safeguards (UK adequacy decisions, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, or Standard Contractual Clauses).

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as is required by UK tax and accounting law (typically six years from the end of the accounting period the order falls into). Marketing email subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe. Other data is deleted when it's no longer needed, or on request (where we are legally allowed to delete it).

Cookies

This website uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Strictly necessary cookies, for the basket, checkout, login state and security. These are always on, because the site can't function without them.
  • Analytics cookies (if enabled), to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it. We will only set these where you have consented.
  • Marketing / preference cookies (if enabled), to remember your choices and (where applicable) to allow third parties to show our products. Only set with consent.

You can control cookies through your browser settings, and (where we offer one) through the cookie banner on the site.

Your rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is used (this policy).
  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Have inaccurate data corrected.
  • Have your data erased (the "right to be forgotten"), where we don't have a legal reason to keep it.
  • Restrict how we process your data.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests, or to direct marketing.
  • Receive your data in a portable format, where it was provided by you and processed by automated means.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we relied on it to process your data.

To exercise any of these rights, email team@willabean.co.uk. We aim to respond within one calendar month.

Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please contact us first so we can try to resolve it. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), the UK's data-protection regulator.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example to reflect new features or changes to data-protection law. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised.
