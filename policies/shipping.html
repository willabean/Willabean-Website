Shipping & Delivery

Last updated: 25 April 2026

The short version. We ship to UK addresses only. Free UK delivery on orders over £75. Stocked branded products dispatched same-day if ordered before 2pm. Handmade collars and leads are made-to-order, expect 7–10 working days build time before dispatch (14–21 days for the Archie).

Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses in the United Kingdom only (England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland). We do not ship internationally at this time.

If you are based outside the UK and would like to order, please email team@willabean.co.uk first and we'll see what we can arrange on a case-by-case basis.

Build times before dispatch

Most of our products are handmade to order in our UK workshop. The build time varies by product:

  • Bertie, Hugo, Walter, Winnie, Mabel and Willow collars and leads, 7 to 10 working days from order to dispatch.
  • Archie bespoke collars and leads, 14 to 21 working days from order to dispatch.
  • Henry Traffic Handle, 7 to 10 working days from order to dispatch.
  • Mud Daddy and Sporting Saint products, in stock and dispatched same-day if ordered before 2pm on a working day.

Build times are estimates, not guarantees, but we'll always tell you if anything will take longer than the figure above.

Delivery times after dispatch

UK delivery is via Royal Mail (or another tracked courier where appropriate). Typical transit times after dispatch:

  • Royal Mail Tracked 48, 2 to 3 working days.
  • Royal Mail Tracked 24 (where offered), 1 to 2 working days.

Delivery times are estimates provided by the courier and are not guaranteed.

Delivery costs

Standard UK delivery costs are shown at checkout, calculated by order value and weight. Orders over £75 ship free of charge to the UK.

Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you'll receive an email with a tracking link, so you can follow it from our workshop to your door.

If your parcel is delayed or missing

Royal Mail considers a UK parcel "lost" only after 10 working days from dispatch. If your order hasn't arrived in that window, email us at team@willabean.co.uk with your order number and we'll open a trace with the courier. We don't leave you to chase the carrier on your own.

Incorrect address

Please double-check the delivery address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us straight away, we'll correct it if the order hasn't already gone out. Once an order has been dispatched to the address you provided, we can't be held responsible for non-delivery caused by an incorrect address, but we'll do what we can to help you recover the parcel.

Damaged or undelivered items

If your order arrives damaged, see our Returns & Refunds Policy. Take a photo of the packaging before unpacking further if possible, and email us within 48 hours.

Questions about a specific order? Email team@willabean.co.uk with your order number, we'll come back to you, usually the same day.
